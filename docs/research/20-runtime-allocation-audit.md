# Runtime allocation-idiom audit of the hand-written SDK code

Date: 2026-08-24

> Dated evidence and decision history, not current policy. Follow current canon through
> `AGENTS.md`.

This audit answers one question: how thoroughly does the hand-written runtime already use
low/no-allocation .NET idioms, and where are the misses — including idioms beyond the obvious
Span/readonly-struct/stackalloc trio. It audits the **committed state** (`HEAD`, `1222664`): the
four dirty worktree files (`Pipeline.cs`, `ResponseBodyReader.cs`, `ResponseEncodingPolicy.cs`,
`EncodedResponseBody.cs`) were read via `git show HEAD:` and are cited as `HEAD:<path>:<line>`;
the untracked `PooledResponseBody*`/`TrackingByteArrayPool` experiment files are excluded
entirely. Scope: every non-generated file under `src/OpenCode.Sdk` and
`src/OpenCode.Sdk.Extensions` (37 files; generated files carry the
`Generated by OpenCode.Sdk.Tools` header), a pattern sample of the generated output (tagged
**emitter-fixable** — generated files change only through the generator), and a light pass over
`tools/OpenCode.Sdk.Tools` (tagged **tool-lowprio**).

Method: the `analyzing-dotnet-performance` checklist (~50 anti-patterns across async, memory,
strings, collections, LINQ, regex, serialization, I/O) extended with closure/delegate capture,
params arrays, enumerator boxing, string-building strategy, struct copying, capacity presizing,
`ToArray`/`ToList` churn, async state-machine shape, and exception-as-control-flow. API
availability facts per TFM are **not re-derived** here; they are cross-referenced to
`docs/research/17-modern-dotnet-allocation-apis.md` (doc 17). Findings are weighted by the
measured attribution: materialization is 88–96% of one-shot elapsed from 2 KB up (Q121), the
pipeline read touches 2.0x wire bytes, the SSE reader carries a ~26–52 KB fixed cost per
enumeration, and the health-call floor is ~1.8 KB. A miss on materialization, SSE framing, body
read, or per-request route/query building outranks construction-time code. Per
`docs/engineering/quality-gates.md`, unbenchmarked cost estimates below are code-reading claims;
any acted-on finding settles its numbers in `tests/OpenCode.Sdk.Performance.Tests` first.

Increment tags follow the sealed policy-pipeline plan (session 40): `increment-2` skeleton,
`increment-2b` A3-Full verdicts (generator), `increment-3` pooled buffering + progress timeout,
`increment-4` encoding hardening, `sse-stage-2` byte-level SSE framing.

## Positives — the baseline the findings sit on

- **UTF-8 end-to-end on the dominant one-shot path.** Valid-UTF-8 bodies stay bytes:
  `EncodedResponseBody.Utf8Body` (`HEAD:src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/EncodedResponseBody.cs:7`) →
  `adapter.AdaptSuccess(status, encodedBody.Utf8Body.Span)`
  (`HEAD:src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Pipeline.cs:247`) → the `ReadOnlySpan<byte>` deserialize
  overload (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ResponseAdapter.cs:50-63`). No UTF-16 detour where the
  88–96% of elapsed lives.
- **`readonly record struct` carriers, and no non-readonly structs anywhere.**
  `EncodedResponseBody` (`HEAD:…/EncodedResponseBody.cs:7`), `ServerSentEvent`
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ServerSentEvent.cs:11`). Zero defensive-copy exposure in the
  hand-written scope.
- **`MemoryMarshal.TryGetArray` instead of `ToArray`** for the decoded-body fallback
  (`HEAD:…/EncodedResponseBody.cs:21-23`); the `ToArray()` branch is reachable only for
  non-array-backed memory, which the pipeline never constructs.
- **Request bodies serialize straight to UTF-8**: `JsonSerializer.SerializeToUtf8Bytes` with
  source-gen `JsonTypeInfo` (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:266`); JSON-as-string appears nowhere on the
  send path.
- **Source-gen STJ exclusively.** Every serialize/deserialize call passes `JsonTypeInfo`
  (`OpenCodeJsonContext`); the `new JsonSerializerOptions` recipe hit zero.
- **Span scanning in the SSE reader**: `remaining.IndexOfAny('\r', '\n')`
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ServerSentEventReader.cs:146-147`), and
  `stream.ReadAsync(bytes.AsMemory(), ct)` (`…/ServerSentEventReader.cs:82`) — the `Memory<byte>`
  overload is copy-free downlevel through Polyfill's `MemoryMarshal.TryGetArray` wrapper (doc 17
  §3).
- **Reused mutable state in the SSE reader**: field `StringBuilder`s cleared per frame
  (`…/ServerSentEventReader.cs:23-24,200,310`), a static strict-UTF8 encoding (`:21`), and a
  per-TFM adapter for value append — modern `Append(StringBuilder,int,int)` versus a reused
  downlevel copy buffer (`:262-282`) — exactly the house algorithm-divergence rule.
- **`Utf8JsonReader` discipline in union dispatch**: struct-reader copy for lookahead
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Serialization/UnionDiscriminatorReader.cs:62`), `ValueTextEquals`
  for property names (`:72`) and expected markers (`:40`), `TrySkip` (`:84`) — known-tag payloads
  never touch a DOM.
- **Singleton discipline everywhere construction repeats**: response/stream adapter `Instance`
  properties (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ResponseAdapters/HealthResponseAdapter.cs:20`,
  `…/StreamAdapters/EventSubscribeResponseStreamAdapter.cs:22`), static per-status tag arrays
  (`HealthResponseAdapter.cs:11-12` — the reason the `params` recipe hit zero),
  `UserAgentPolicy.Resolved` running its reflection once per process
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/UserAgentPolicy.cs:13-17`), `OpenCodeRequestOptions.NoThrow`
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/OpenCodeRequestOptions.cs:10`), `SessionParentFilter.RootOnly`
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Sessions/SessionParentFilter.cs:15`), `UnionPayloadGuard.Instance`
  (`…/Serialization/UnionPayloadGuard.cs:12`), cached `EmptyCause`
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/OpenCodeStreamFailureException.cs:8-9`).
- **Closure hygiene**: every hot- or warm-path lambda is `static` —
  `HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:468` (stream-cancellation dispose), `HEAD:…/ResponseBodyReader.cs:75-79`
  (fault observation), `src/OpenCode.Sdk.Extensions/OpenCodeServiceCollectionExtensions.cs:49-52`
  (DI factories). No capturing lambdas exist on any request path.
- **Empty-value idioms**: `body = []` → `Array.Empty<byte>`
  (`HEAD:…/ResponseBodyReader.cs:17`), empty-span success adapt `AdaptSuccess(status, [])`
  (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:240`), lazy `_builder ??= new StringBuilder()` so query-less requests
  allocate nothing (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/QueryStringBuilder.cs:84`), `path + ""` collapsing
  to `path` via `string.Concat` identity.
- **Transport idioms**: one owned `HttpClient` with `PooledConnectionLifetime`
  (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:185-189`) / ServicePoint lease downlevel (`:341-346`);
  `HttpCompletionOption.ResponseHeadersRead` on every send (`:308`); `Stopwatch.GetTimestamp`
  instead of a `Stopwatch` instance (`:231,335`); DI registers singletons only.
- **String hygiene**: `StringComparison.Ordinal`/`OrdinalIgnoreCase` on every comparison the
  recipes can reach; zero `Substring`, `ToLower`, `string.Format`, regex, or LINQ on any runtime
  path; hand-rolled allocation-free scans where the BCL would allocate
  (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/RouteValuePolicy.cs:37-58` UTF-16 validity;
  `HEAD:…/ResponseEncodingPolicy.cs:107-132` BOM prefix checks on scalar bytes).
- **Guard clauses via throw-helper statics** (`ArgumentNullException.ThrowIfNull`,
  `ArgumentException.ThrowIfNullOrWhiteSpace`, `ObjectDisposedException.ThrowIf`) — no message
  formatting on the happy path; exception construction is confined to failure branches.
- **`ConfigureAwait(false)` product-wide**, triple-enforced by analyzers
  (`docs/engineering/quality-gates.md`), and zero sync-over-async anywhere.

## Findings

Severity: **Critical** (dominant measured cost), **Moderate** (clear win on a hot path),
**Info** (real but small, or honest negative). Every finding names its absorbing increment or an
explicit verdict.

### A. One-shot body intake (hot: 2.0x wire bytes, materialization-dominant)

**A1. Whole-body `ReadAsByteArrayAsync` — unpooled growth plus an escaping exact-size array.**
Critical. `HEAD:src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ResponseBodyReader.cs:22` (modern) and `:25`
(downlevel). `HttpContent` buffers into an unpooled doubling stream on net8/net9/net472 and then
copies out via `ToArray()` (doc 17 §2) — this is the measured 2.0x wire-byte amplification of the
pipeline read. Fix: `ResponseBufferingPolicy` owning an `ArrayPool<byte>`-backed contiguous
buffer fed by `HttpContent.CopyToAsync`, `Content-Length` as a clamped initial-rent hint,
exposing `ReadOnlyMemory<byte>` and never an escaping array (net10 `LimitArrayPoolWriteStream`
blueprint). TFM: `System.Buffers` 4.6.1 already in the downlevel graph; same algorithm on all
five targets. Tag: **increment-3** (planned; R16 mechanism accepted).

**A2. `Task.WaitAsync` per body read as the timeout mechanism.**
Moderate. `HEAD:…/ResponseBodyReader.cs:29-36`. Each read allocates a timeout promise plus timer
registration on modern .NET, and a `TaskCompletionSource` + timer through the Polyfill shim
downlevel; on timeout the underlying read is abandoned (content disposed, fault observed at
`:52-64,68-80`) rather than interrupted. Fix: one linked CTS per operation with `CancelAfter`
re-armed per observed read — allocation-free after the first arm on every TFM — and
dispose-to-interrupt as the downlevel mechanism (doc 17 §4; `HttpClient`'s own timeout shape).
TFM: algorithm divergence → per-TFM adapter seam, per house rule. Tag: **increment-3** (planned).

**A3. Task-shaped internal seams; `ValueTask` absent from the codebase.**
Moderate. Grep: 0 hand-written `ValueTask`. `ResponseBodyReader.ReadAsync`
(`HEAD:…/ResponseBodyReader.cs:8`) allocates a `Task<EncodedResponseBody>` + state-machine box
per response; the same shape will apply to each `PipelinePolicy.ProcessAsync` hop. Fix: keep hot
internal seams `ValueTask`-shaped where synchronous completion occurs (doc 17 §5: default to
`Task` on public surfaces; do not contort downlevel — Polyfill's downlevel `ValueTask` wraps the
Task anyway, shape not savings). Decide the seam shape when the policy roster is introduced.
Tag: **increment-2** skeleton.

### B. SSE framing and stream materialization (hot: ~26–52 KB fixed + per-frame churn)

**B1. Decode-to-UTF-16-then-scan, then re-transcode to UTF-8 for materialization.**
Critical. The committed reader decodes every payload byte to chars
(`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ServerSentEventReader.cs:60-62,71`), accumulates them in
`StringBuilder`s, allocates a payload `string` per frame (`:215`, `_data.ToString()`), and the
pipeline then deserializes that **string** (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:498,512`), which STJ transcodes
back to UTF-8 internally — every payload byte crosses the UTF-16 boundary twice. The unpooled
fixed cost is the measured ~26–52 KB per enumeration: `new byte[8192]` (`:61`), a ~16 KB
`char[]` (`:62`), plus a second ~16 KB downlevel copy buffer (`:64`). Fix: byte-level framing per
the dotnet/runtime `SseParser` blueprint — pooled contiguous line buffer, byte
`IndexOfAny((byte)'\r', (byte)'\n')`, CR-deferral, BOM skip, payload handed as
`ReadOnlySpan<byte>` straight into the `Utf8JsonReader`-based deserialize; strictness delta
(`Utf8.IsValid` per payload vs replacement parity) decided explicitly (doc 17 §3). TFM: byte
scan is vectorized modern, scalar downlevel; the allocation wins transfer, throughput claims
need the net472 leg. Tag: **sse-stage-2** (planned).

**B2. Union discriminator materializes one tag `string` per payload.**
Moderate, hot (every SSE event and every union-typed value inside one-shot bodies).
`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Serialization/UnionDiscriminatorReader.cs:18` (`marker.GetString()`)
feeding `TypesByTag.TryGetValue(marker, …)` in the generated converters
(`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Serialization/EventJsonConverter.cs:106-107`). For known tags the
string exists only to key a dictionary. Fix sketch: on net9+, keep `Dictionary<string,…>` and
use `GetAlternateLookup<ReadOnlySpan<char>>()` with `reader.CopyString` into a `stackalloc
char[…]` buffer (tags are short ASCII); the allocation remains only on the unknown-tag path,
which needs the string anyway. TFM: alternate lookup is net9+ inbox-only → `#if
NET9_0_OR_GREATER`, downlevel keeps `GetString`. Tag: **emitter-fixable** (converter emission)
plus a small hand-written reader API addition (**new-candidate**).

**B3. Per-enumeration reader/closure overhead.** Info. One `ServerSentEventReader` +
`GetAsyncEnumerator` chain per stream (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:401-404`) and one compiler display
class for the `Decode` local function capturing `decoder`/`bytes`/`characters` across awaits
(`…/ServerSentEventReader.cs:67-77`). A handful of objects per enumeration against the 26–52 KB
buffers — irrelevant standalone, and the stage-2 rewrite deletes the code. Tag: **not-worth-it**
standalone; absorbed by **sse-stage-2**.

### C. Encoding policy (per one-shot response)

**C1. Exception-as-control-flow UTF-8 validation, and a discarded full decode pass.**
Moderate. `HEAD:src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/ResponseEncodingPolicy.cs:37-49`: `IsValidUtf8` runs
strict `GetCharCount` over the whole body (a full decode pass whose only output is a discarded
count) and catches `DecoderFallbackException` to signal invalidity. Fix: `Utf8.IsValid` — exact
same predicate, exception-free, no char counting (doc 17 §1). TFM: net8+ inbox; downlevel
requires adding `Microsoft.Bcl.Memory` (open maintainer dependency decision) or keeping the
strict-decoder try/catch behind the per-TFM adapter. Tag: **increment-4** (planned).

**C2. Quoted-charset `charset[1..^1]` substring.** Info.
`HEAD:…/ResponseEncodingPolicy.cs:57-58`. Reachable only when a server sends a *quoted* charset;
`HttpContent.ReadBufferAsString` allocates identically, so parity does not require removing it
(doc 17 §1). Tag: **not-worth-it** (parity-conformant; optional well-known-charset span fast
path already catalogued in doc 17 belongs to increment-4 if ever wanted).

**C3. `GetPreamble()` called twice in the default arm.** Info.
`HEAD:…/ResponseEncodingPolicy.cs:104`: `StartsWith(body, encoding.GetPreamble()) ?
encoding.GetPreamble().Length : 0` — `Encoding.GetPreamble` returns a fresh array per call, so a
declared non-UTF8/16/32 charset pays two array copies. Fix: one local. Reachable only for exotic
declared charsets. Tag: trivially absorbed by **increment-4**; not worth a standalone change.

### D. Request build path (per request; small against the ~1.8 KB floor)

**D1. Route/query composition allocates a handful of small objects per parameterized request.**
Info→Moderate only in aggregate. Per request with parameters: segment concat
(`src/OpenCode.Sdk/OpenCodeRoutes.cs:61,97,138`), a `QueryStringBuilder` **class** instance
(`OpenCodeRoutes.cs:67,144`), an unsized `StringBuilder` (default 16-char first chunk;
`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/QueryStringBuilder.cs:84`), the final `ToString` (`:14`), and the
`path + query.Value` concat (`OpenCodeRoutes.cs:70,148`) — roughly 3–5 small allocations, a few
percent of the 1.8 KB health floor. `RouteValuePolicy.EscapeName` over SDK-literal names
(`QueryStringBuilder.cs:86,99`) is CPU-only — `Uri.EscapeDataString` returns the input string
unchanged when nothing needs escaping on all TFMs — but it rescans a compile-time constant per
request. Fix sketches (all emitter side): pre-escape names at generation time; presize
(`new StringBuilder(64)`) or make the builder a struct passed by ref; compose fixed-shape routes
with `string.Create`/interpolation handlers on `#if NET`. Tag: **emitter-fixable**, low
priority — do not act without a benchmark showing route building visible next to
materialization.

**D2. Per-request location-header escaping of an immutable snapshot.**
Moderate (only when `Location` is configured), and free to fix.
`HEAD:src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Pipeline.cs:284-292`: `Decorate` runs
`Uri.EscapeDataString(directory)` on **every request**, yet `_location` is a validated immutable
record snapshot taken at construction (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/LocationSelector.cs:10-44`; the
snapshot contract is stated at `HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:65-66`). Fix: precompute the escaped header
value once in the constructor alongside `_authorization`, which already models exactly this
(`:67-69`). No TFM caveat. Tag: **new-candidate** — fold into increment-2's RequestDecoration
policy.

**D3. `new MediaTypeHeaderValue("application/json")` per body-bearing request.**
Info. `HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:267`. The repo already shares one immutable-by-discipline header value
process-wide (`UserAgentPolicy.Resolved`, `src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/UserAgentPolicy.cs:13-17`),
so a `static readonly MediaTypeHeaderValue` is consistent — with the caveat that the type is
mutable (`CharSet` setter): sharing is safe only under internal discipline that nothing mutates
it. Tag: **new-candidate** (tiny), same RequestDecoration home as D2.

**D4. `SerializeToUtf8Bytes` escaping array + `ByteArrayContent` per request body.**
Info / honest negative. `HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:264-269`. One exact-size UTF-8 array per body — the
send path is not where the benchmarks attribute cost, request bodies are small, and a pooled
writer + `ReadOnlyMemoryContent` would add ownership complexity for an unmeasured win. Tag:
**not-worth-it** now; revisit only with send-path benchmark evidence.

**D5. `request ?? new SessionCreateRequest()` allocates an empty record per omitted-body call.**
Info. `src/OpenCode.Sdk/Sessions/SessionsClient.cs:57` (pattern repeats across generated
clients). Records are immutable; a cached static empty instance removes the allocation. Tag:
**emitter-fixable**, trivial.

### E. Cold paths, error paths, structure (honest negatives)

**E1. Union payload double scan.** Info. `UnionDiscriminatorReader.Find` pre-scans the object
with a struct-reader copy (`…/UnionDiscriminatorReader.cs:55-96`), then the converter re-parses
the same tokens through `JsonSerializer.Deserialize(ref reader, typeInfo)`
(`EventJsonConverter.cs:110`). Zero allocation (the copies are stack struct copies — the idiom
is correct); the double token walk is CPU inherent to discriminator-outside-metadata dispatch,
and STJ's own polymorphism buffers similarly. Tag: **not-worth-it** (mechanism note so it is not
re-audited).

**E2. Unknown-tag path builds the DOM twice.** Info. `EventJsonConverter.cs:113-115` parses a
`JsonDocument`, and the carrier clones the element to outlive the `using`
(`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Models/UnknownEvent.cs:29`; same shape in all 17 `Unknown*` carriers —
verified, so no disposed-document bug exists). Two DOM copies per **unknown** tag only; the
clone is the ownership contract. Tag: **not-worth-it** (rare path, correctness-motivated).

**E3. `OpenCodeErrorReader.Downgrade` re-parses the raw body.** Info.
`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/OpenCodeErrorReader.cs:50-51` — second parse of an error body that
was already deserialized once at `:22`. Error path; raw-body retention on errors is a declared
contract cost (`quality-gates.md`), not an optimization target. Tag: **not-worth-it**.

**E4. `JsonException` catch as tolerant-read control flow.** Info.
`…/OpenCodeErrorReader.cs:31-34`, `ResponseAdapter.cs:43-47,59-63`,
`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:501-505,515-519`. All confined to failure/protocol-error branches; the
happy path never throws. Deliberate fail-loud posture. Tag: **not-worth-it**.

**E5. `Enumerable.Contains` with comparer on the typed-error path.** Info.
`…/OpenCodeErrorReader.cs:27` — the comparer overload always enumerates, allocating an array
enumerator per typed-error read. Error path; a `foreach` over the (always tiny) tag array fixes
it if the code is ever touched — the 2b verdict regeneration rewrites this neighborhood anyway.
Tag: **not-worth-it** standalone; note for **increment-2b**.

**E6. Pagination enumerator + delegate.** Info.
`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Pagination/CursorPaginator.cs:35` — `foreach` over
`IReadOnlyList<TItem>` allocates an interface enumerator per **page** (an indexed `for` would
not); `src/OpenCode.Sdk/Sessions/SessionClient.cs:107` — the method-group `Func` conversion
allocates one delegate per traversal. One-to-two objects per page against a full page
materialization. Tag: **not-worth-it** (recorded so it is not re-found).

**E7. Unsealed classes are all deliberate.** Info. The only unsealed non-abstract hand-written
classes are the public exception hierarchy (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/OpenCodeException.cs:4`,
`OpenCodeApiException.cs:6`, `OpenCodeTransportException.cs:4` — the latter is subclassed by
`OpenCodeStreamFailureException`); generated client classes are unsealed-with-virtuals as the
documented mock seam (`src/OpenCode.Sdk/OpenCodeClient.cs:10,72-81`). Devirtualization is
consciously traded for the mocking contract; everything internal is sealed. Tag:
**not-worth-it** (design contract).

**E8. Construction-time code is already allocation-frugal.** Positive negative: basic-auth
header built once (`HEAD:…/Pipeline.cs:67-69`), endpoint normalized once
(`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/EndpointPolicy.cs:38`), user-agent reflection once per process. No
findings.

### F. Generated-output patterns (sampled; all emitter-fixable)

**F1. Tag-dispatch tables are `Dictionary`, not `FrozenDictionary`.** Info→Moderate on the SSE
path. `EventJsonConverter.cs:11` (`static readonly Dictionary<string, Type>`, ~90 entries,
Ordinal) — one lookup per event; `FrozenDictionary` is ~50% faster per lookup, zero allocation
delta. TFM: `System.Collections.Frozen` is net8+ inbox; downlevel coverage exists only via the
`System.Collections.Immutable` 8+ package (a dependency decision) — otherwise
`#if NET8_0_OR_GREATER` with the `Dictionary` fallback, zero new dependencies. Tag:
**emitter-fixable**.

**F2. Tag→`Type`→`JsonTypeInfo` double hop per event.** Info. `EventJsonConverter.cs:107-109`
resolves the tag to a `Type`, then `OpenCodeJsonContext.Default.GetTypeInfo(Type)` per event
(cached inside the context, but still a second dictionary probe + null-check throw). The emitter
could build a lazy tag→`JsonTypeInfo` map directly. CPU-only. Tag: **emitter-fixable**, low.

**F3. `JsonSourceGenerationMode.Metadata` only — no fast-path serialization.** Info.
`src/OpenCode.Sdk/Internal/Serialization/OpenCodeJsonContext.cs:8`. Deserialization (the
measured hot direction) is unaffected — fast path is serialize-only — and custom converters
suppress fast path for union types anyway; only plain request-body writes could gain CPU from
`Metadata | Serialization`. Tag: **emitter-fixable**; **not-worth-it** until the send path is
ever measured.

**F4. Route builders and adapters otherwise emit the right idioms.** Positive: `Instance`
singletons, static tag arrays, span-based `AdaptSuccess`, `const` route templates, dot-segment
guards before any allocation (`OpenCodeRoutes.cs:53-70,130-148`). The D1 composition costs are
the only emitter-side allocation pattern found.

### G. Tools

Light pass, 181 files: no egregious patterns (see checklist). The per-generation cost profile
does not justify deeper audit. Tag: **tool-lowprio**, no findings.

## Coverage verdict

The hand-written runtime already sits in the upper tier of the modern low-allocation idiom set,
and — more tellingly — the classic anti-pattern inventory comes back essentially empty: zero
`Substring`/`ToLower`/`string.Format`/regex/params/per-call-collection hits, zero LINQ on
runtime paths, zero sync-over-async, zero uncached `JsonSerializerOptions`, comparisons
ordinal throughout, closures static throughout, singletons wherever construction would repeat.
The idioms that are present are the load-bearing ones: UTF-8 bytes end-to-end on the dominant
one-shot success path, `readonly record struct` carriers, `MemoryMarshal.TryGetArray`,
span scanning and `Memory<byte>` stream reads in the SSE reader, `Utf8JsonReader`
`ValueTextEquals`/`TrySkip` union dispatch without a DOM, source-generated STJ exclusively, and
disciplined per-TFM adapters where downlevel diverges. What is *absent* is equally coherent:
`ArrayPool`, pooled buffering, `ValueTask`, `Utf8.IsValid`, `FrozenDictionary`, alternate
lookups, `stackalloc`, and `string.Create` appear nowhere — and that absence maps almost exactly
onto the two big measured costs (unpooled body intake at 2.0x wire bytes; SSE's UTF-16 detour
with ~26–52 KB fixed buffers) that increments 3/4 and SSE stage 2 were already sealed to
rebuild, plus a short tail of per-request micro-allocations (location-header escaping, media-type
header, route composition, union tag strings) that are either one-line constructor hoists or
emitter changes gated on net8/net9 APIs. `SearchValues` is correctly absent (doc 17: no
multi-value scan exists to justify it). Net: this is not a codebase that needs an allocation
culture change; it needs the two planned structural rebuilds to land, and the small
new-candidate/emitter items above folded into the increments that already own their
neighborhoods.
